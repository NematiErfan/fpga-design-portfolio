# FPGA & Digital Systems Engineering

I am an FPGA and Digital Systems Engineer with an academic background in Computer Systems Architecture and experience in hardware development, RTL design, and engineering R&D.

My core expertise is in FPGA-based digital systems, VHDL development, digital signal processing, and embedded hardware architectures. I am interested in designing reliable, efficient, and reusable hardware solutions for real-time processing and control applications.
